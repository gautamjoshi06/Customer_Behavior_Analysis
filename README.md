# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes 3,900 customer transactions to uncover shopping patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discount behavior, and subscription trends using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python:** Pandas, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and feature engineering
- **PostgreSQL:** SQL queries and business analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **Data Analysis:** Customer segmentation, revenue analysis, product ratings, and purchasing behavior

### 📊 Key Features
- Analyzed revenue by gender and age group.
- Compared subscribers and non-subscribers.
- Identified top-rated and best-selling products.
- Evaluated discount usage and discount-dependent products.
- Segmented customers into New, Returning, and Loyal groups.
- Compared Standard and Express shipping purchase amounts.
- Examined the relationship between repeat purchases and subscriptions.
- Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to present business insights.

### 💡 Business Insights
- Male customers generated higher observed revenue than female customers.
- Clothing was the leading product category by revenue.
- Discount usage remained significant, highlighting the need to balance promotions with profitability.
- Subscription adoption was 27%, indicating an opportunity to improve subscriber conversion.
- Express shipping had a slightly higher average purchase amount than Standard shipping.

### 🎯 Business Recommendations
- Improve subscription adoption through exclusive benefits.
- Introduce loyalty rewards to encourage repeat purchases.
- Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Promote top-rated and best-selling products.
- Target high-value customer segments with relevant marketing campaigns.

### 🚀 Project Objective
Transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decisions in customer retention, marketing, merchandising, and revenue growth.
